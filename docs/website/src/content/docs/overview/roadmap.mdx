gvy has one roadmap: the [Quality Bar](https://github.com/albertocavalcante/gvy/blob/main/docs/quality-bar.md).

> **gvy gives correct, honest and fast answers about Groovy code, and never damages it.**

The Quality Bar page states the properties, the scorecard and the tracks of work. Live status is tracked in
[issue #1551](https://github.com/albertocavalcante/gvy/issues/1551), and current LSP support is listed in the
[feature matrix](/gvy/lsp/feature-support/).

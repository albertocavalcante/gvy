gvy has one roadmap: the [North Star](https://github.com/albertocavalcante/gvy/blob/main/docs/north-star.md).

> **gvy gives correct, honest and fast answers about Groovy code, and never damages it.**

The North Star page states the principles, the scorecard and the lanes of work. Live status is tracked in
[issue #1551](https://github.com/albertocavalcante/gvy/issues/1551), and current LSP support is listed in the
[feature matrix](https://github.com/albertocavalcante/gvy/blob/main/docs/lsp/feature-support.md).
